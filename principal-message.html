Principal message

Miss Sabita Bisht: helping every learner grow with confidence.

Principal

At B.R. International School, the Principal's message is centered on one promise: every child deserves a school experience where learning feels purposeful, safe, and encouraging. Children do not grow through pressure alone. They grow through guidance, routine, trust, and meaningful opportunities to express themselves.

Miss Sabita Bisht believes that a classroom should be a place where a child can learn facts, but also learn how to think. A good lesson builds knowledge. A good teacher builds confidence. A good school builds both, while also teaching children how to behave with respect, how to participate with courage, and how to keep improving even when something feels difficult.

The Principal's approach brings academics and activities together. Strong classroom learning remains important, but sports, cultural programs, celebrations, parade participation, and group activities are also part of complete development. These experiences help students speak, cooperate, lead, listen, and discover talents that may not appear in a textbook.

Discipline at the school is guided by care. Students are encouraged to understand why good habits matter. Cleanliness, punctuality, respectful speech, regular study, teamwork, and responsibility become part of everyday school life. These habits prepare children not only for exams, but for the world beyond school.

The Principal also values the partnership between teachers and parents. When school and home work together, children feel more secure and more motivated. A child who feels supported is more willing to try, more willing to ask, and more willing to learn from mistakes.

The message for every student is simple and inspiring: keep learning, keep participating, keep respecting others, and keep believing that small daily efforts can create a strong future. B.R. International School is committed to walking with students through that journey.
