About the school

A purposeful institution built to shape capable, compassionate, quality individuals.

Founded by Babu Ram Verma, father of Mr. Uma Shanker, the school was established at Vinay Vihar, Sugamau, Indira Nagar, Lucknow with a clear mission: open doors to opportunity through meaningful education.

This value-led learning environment gives children in Lucknow a safe, inspiring path for academic progress, confidence, and character.

"Education is the most powerful weapon which you can use to change the world."

Nelson Mandela

"He who opens a school door, closes a prison."

Victor Hugo

Our motive

  • To help every child grow into a total quality person.
  • To develop explorers by nurturing each child's underlying capabilities.
  • To treat curricular and co-curricular activities as essential to complete development.

Our students have excelled in academics, Republic Day Parade participation, annual sports, and cultural events, bringing pride to themselves and to the school community while growing through safe, inspiring school life.

Leadership messages

Meet the school leaders who guide B.R. International School with discipline, values, and a student-first approach.

Manager message and journey

Mr. Uma Shanker

Manager

B.R. International School began with a simple purpose: to open the door of meaningful education for children in Sugamau, Indira Nagar, Lucknow. From a small beginning with five students and five teachers, the journey has grown through trust, discipline, and steady commitment.

Our focus remains on helping every child become confident, capable, and rooted in values. We believe education should build character, encourage curiosity, and prepare students to contribute positively to family, society, and the nation.

Learn more about the Manager journey Open leadership blog

Principal message

Miss Sabita Bisht

Principal

At B.R. International School, learning is planned with care so that every child feels seen, supported, and encouraged. Our classrooms aim to balance strong academics with activities, cultural participation, sports, and everyday discipline.

We work to create a school environment where students can ask questions, build confidence, respect others, and grow beyond textbooks. With the support of teachers and families, every learner can move forward with purpose.
